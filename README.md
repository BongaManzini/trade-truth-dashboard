# 🇿🇦 Trade Truth: South Africa & United States Bilateral Economic Reality

> **Live Application**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/trade-truth-dashboard/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/trade-truth-dashboard/)

An interactive macroeconomic trade analytics dashboard tracking the actual economic relationship between South Africa and the United States, dismantling viral panic regarding immediate sanctions, AGOA expulsion, and aid cutoffs.

---

## 🎯 The Informatics & Economic Reality Angle

- **The Problem**: Recurring viral rumors on financial forums and social networks claim that *"the US is sanctioning South Africa tomorrow"* and that *"the Rand will collapse to R30/USD because all aid is cancelled."*
- **The Empirical Reality**:
  - **AGOA Duty-Free Status**: Fully active and operational, protecting zero-tariff exports for South African automotive assembly (BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Ford), citrus growers, and wine producers.
  - **Trade Surplus**: South Africa exports **$14.2 Billion (approx. R265 Billion ZAR)** in goods annually to the US (primarily Platinum Group Metals, catalytic converters, and manufacturing components), while importing $8.5 Billion—maintaining a **healthy trade surplus** in South Africa's favor.
  - **Aid Specificity**: Foreign aid (such as the $400M annual PEPFAR grant) goes directly to non-governmental HIV/AIDS clinical research partners and antiretroviral delivery channels, representing less than **0.3% of South Africa's national budget**.

---

## 🛠️ Features

- **Macroeconomic KPI Cards**: Real-time status indicators for AGOA enrollment, bilateral trade balance, and sanctions truth.
- **Top Export Breakdown**: Visualized trade shares across Platinum Group Metals (28.8%), Motor Vehicles (19.7%), Agricultural Citrus/Wine (9.8%), and Raw Minerals (8.4%).
- **Interactive Panic vs. Data Fact-Checker**: Directly addresses and clarifies common legislative misconceptions.
